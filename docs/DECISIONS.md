# Product decisions

One line each, dated. Claude reads this before asking questions.

- 2026-10-21: «درخواست تازه» SMS capped at 3 per hour per client and provider (user approved).
- 2026-10-21: A service duration over 720 minutes is clamped to 720, not rejected (user approved).
- 2026-10-21: Changing a phone number always needs an SMS code for the new number (user approved).
- 2026-10-21: No online deposit in-app for now; idea under study: each salon/artist connects their own gateway (Bale Pay) for optional deposits. Not started; see docs/ROADMAP.md when added.
- 2026-10-21: Anti-abuse plan (user wants nothing complex for clients): small caps for new clients and a signal panel for the salon; no mandatory client re-confirmation.
- 2026-10-21: Capacity (Package 1): places come from the working artists on the team; one lock per salon and day picks the artist, so n simultaneous requests fill n free artists. A walk-in typed without an artist's name takes one seat. A request is refused when the salon has a team but nobody is working (all on leave or off). The «ظرفیت روز» number in the hours settings is no longer used (kept stored and shown, ignored by bookings).
- 2026-10-21: Deadlines (Package 1): whether a request is still alive is worked out at the moment of asking, not by the 5-minute sweep; approving after its deadline, after the appointment started, or after the day was closed / the hours shrunk / the service deleted is refused. A new time the salon offers is held 6 hours, then the slot is freed, the booking closes and both sides get a push (no new SMS template). The 2026-10-18 rule on overnight requests is unchanged (the hour is not counted in working hours only).
- 2026-10-21: Request caps (Package 1): at most 3 open requests per client per salon/artist and 5 in total; the refusal says «اول یکی از درخواست‌های قبلی را لغو کن یا منتظر جواب بمان». The default wait is 1 hour; «تا هر وقت» lasts until the appointment but at most 24 hours.
- 2026-10-21: Identical requests (Package 1): the same client asking the same provider for the same service, day and time again (double tap, slow network, parallel) gets the existing request back (nothing new, nobody notified twice). No «ادامه؟» prompt, and overlapping requests at different providers (or different times) stay allowed.
- 2026-10-21: Limits (Package 1): at most 100 services per salon or artist; a client can only book a service on the provider's own menu and the length is the provider's; the client's name in the provider's SMS is cut to 30 characters; the free-times list for a client reads only the live bookings of the next 60 days and is cached for 5 seconds.
- 2026-10-22: G1: booking an independent artist needs a login (guest gets 401 «برای رزرو، شمارهٔ موبایلت را وارد کن» and is sent to the sign-up hand-off, then back to the artist's services); the client's name and phone always come from the account (typed ones are ignored, for salons too); same open-request caps as salons (3 per provider, 5 in total), the artist-side refusal says «چند درخواست باز داری. اول جواب یکی را بگیر یا لغوش کن.»; the 20/min throttle is per account (guests per address); the artist's own panel can still enter walk-ins.
- 2026-10-22: G5: a suspended account leaves the directory, name search and its public page (page/API: «این صفحه فعلاً در دسترس نیست.»), a closed one (billing on and a gateway ready) leaves directory and search; neither takes new requests and free-times lists are empty; already confirmed bookings stay and pending requests expire on their normal deadline.
- 2026-10-22: G6: one phone normaliser (Persian, Arabic-Indic, Latin digits; separators dropped) in `app/shared/lib/phone.js` and `frfro_phone_key()` in the database (migration 045 rebuilds phone_normalized); a booking is tied to an account only by its normalised phone, never by name; when an account is deleted its phone is cut off its old bookings; walk-in form says «بدون شماره، مشتری یادآوری پیامکی نمی‌گیرد و نوبت به حسابی وصل نمی‌شود.»
- 2026-10-22: G7: an independent artist's working hours, closed weekdays and the 30-minute step are enforced on the server like a salon's («آرتیست در این ساعت کار نمی‌کند.» / «ساعت را از میان گزینه‌های نشان‌داده‌شده انتخاب کن.»); the booking page offers only free in-hours times; a visit past midnight blocks the early hours of the next day.
- 2026-10-22: G2 (account-security leaks), G3 (removed team members) and G4 (deleted salons) are postponed by the owner; G2 is to be reviewed in detail after the rest.
- 2026-10-22: Rule «booking never broken without notice» (owner, six rules). Q1: a salon or artist cannot delete their account, or leave the team, while a confirmed booking starts within the next 24 hours (until that booking is done or cancelled); the server refuses with the list, only a per-booking cancel with notice to the client is allowed; with no such booking, delete with one confirmation as before. Dialog: «۲ مشتری تا ۲۴ ساعت دیگر نوبت دارند. تا زمانی که این نوبت‌ها انجام یا لغو نشوند نمی‌شود حساب را حذف کرد.» + the list with «تماس با مشتری» and «لغو با اطلاع مشتری».
- 2026-10-22: Q2: changing the time of a CONFIRMED booking is a proposal («پیشنهاد ساعت دیگر»): the client gets push + in-app card + SMS «سالن شیدا می‌خواهد نوبتت را از ۱۰ به ۱۲ ببرد. قبول می‌کنی؟» with «قبول می‌کنم» / «لغو نوبت، بدون هیچ مشکلی» / «تماس با سالن»; the ORIGINAL time stays valid until the client accepts; no answer in 6 hours (or until 2 hours before the booking, the earlier) keeps the original and tells the provider to call the client; the proposed time must be a free in-hours slot (checked again when the client accepts); all under row locks.
- 2026-10-22: Q3: a provider cancelling a confirmed booking that starts in under 24 hours first sees «اول با مشتری تماس بگیر. بعد لغو کن.» with the client's number; the client then gets (push, in-app note, SMS template booking_cancelled_by_provider) «سالن شیدا نوبت تو را لغو کرد. بابت این اتفاق متأسفیم. می‌توانی از فرفرو نوبت دیگری بگیری.»; one SMS per booking (the same claim as booking_answer, never both).
- 2026-10-22: Q4: every cancellation of a CONFIRMED booking by its provider adds 1 to that account's counter (users.provider_cancel_count); shown as the column «لغو توسط سالن» in the admin accounts list; count only, no automatic penalty or message.
- 2026-10-22: Q5: an artist leaving or removed from a salon team follows the same 24-hour rule as Q1; farther confirmed bookings are, one by one, either handed to another artist («واگذار به آرتیست دیگر», the client must accept like Q2) or cancelled with notice («لغو با اطلاع مشتری»); their calendar copies of cancelled bookings disappear at once; waiting requests of that artist go back to the salon unassigned.
- 2026-10-22: Q6: deleting a whole salon follows the same 24-hour rule; farther bookings are cancelled together by «لغو همهٔ نوبت‌ها و ارسال پیامک» (SMS follows the per-booking claim and the push-instead-of-SMS rule); the client then sees «سالن دیگر در فرفرو نیست» on them; the admin can list and cancel the bookings of a deleted salon; calendar copies of a deleted salon never turn into direct artist bookings (they are deleted with it).
- 2026-10-22: A salon or artist whose subscription lapsed stays viewable (page opens, shows «نوبت آنلاین فعلاً بسته است»), is out of the directory and search and takes no new online bookings; no change needed (owner's choice: clients with a booking or a saved link still see the address and phone, and the provider is pushed to renew).
- 2026-10-22: (built, small choices under Q1-Q6) «لغو نوبت، بدون هیچ مشکلی» is only an answer to an OPEN proposal: it takes the proposal first, so a stale tap after the client accepted or the salon withdrew it cancels nothing (409 «پیشنهادی برای این نوبت نمانده است»); the salon can take its proposal back («پس گرفتن پیشنهاد»); the admin deleting an account is not held by the 24-hour block but every open booking is still cancelled with notice; an unapproved SMS template sends nothing and nothing breaks (the push and the in-app card always go).
- 2026-10-22: (built) screens: the proposal screens (button «پیشنهاد ساعت دیگر», «پس گرفتن پیشنهاد», the call-first cancel panel) are built for salons; an independent artist's own cancel of a confirmed booking and their «پیشنهاد ساعت دیگر» button are in docs/ROADMAP.md (the server already supports both).
- 2026-10-22: Group booking (owner approved, defaults taken): a client's request at a salon can be for 1, 2 or 3 people at the same time («برای چند نفر؟», default 1); only hours where that many artists are free together are offered («ساعت‌هایی که برای ۲ نفر با هم جا دارد»); it is ONE request that the salon answers once (counts as one of the client's open requests; the salon's list shows «… · ۲ نفر»); the check and the booking happen under the same row locks as any request, so it is all or nothing («این ساعت دیگر برای ۲ نفر جا ندارد؛ ساعت‌ها به‌روز شد.», nothing is booked, no artist calendar copy is left). Each person gets the same services; at most 8 services in all, so 4 per person for 2 people and 2 per person for 3. A backup salon is not offered for a group together. Only clients ask for groups (the salon's and the artist's own entry forms are unchanged, see ROADMAP).
- 2026-10-22: Group with no common hour: «آره، پشت‌سرهم» turns it into the existing multi-service visit with the services repeated one after another (same salon, one request, no head count); nothing new on the server.
- 2026-10-22: Silent salon (owner approved): an expired salon request whose appointment is still ahead shows «همین نوبت را جای دیگر بگیر» (lists the places the client is connected to that do every one of these services; a tap opens that place's booking form with services, day and hour filled in) and «دوباره از همین سالن بپرس» (a fresh request for the same time, same checks and the same limits of 3 open per place and 5 in all; the server's own words if it is refused). No connected place: «هنوز جای دیگری وصل نکرده‌ای. شمارهٔ سالن یا آرتیست بعدی را بزن.» with the ways to connect. A request already sent on to a backup salon shows neither. Salon requests only; an unanswered direct artist request is in ROADMAP.
- 2026-10-22: No hour on the chosen day (owner approved): the form looks ahead through the next 14 days (the days shown) and says «اولین ساعت خالی: دوشنبه ۴ آبان ۱۰:۳۰» with «برو به آن روز»; a closed day can be tapped and says «این روز سالن تعطیل است. روز بعد باز: شنبه.»; nothing for 14 days says «تا دو هفته آینده جا نیست. به سالن پیام بده» with «تماس» only when the salon's number is shown on its page.
- 2026-10-22: Hour taken while sending (owner approved): never a silent jump to another hour. The form asks for fresh hours, leaves the hour empty and says «این ساعت همین حالا گرفته شد. ساعت خالیِ بعدی ۱۱:۳۰ است. می‌خواهی همین را بفرستم؟» with «بله، ارسال» / «ساعت دیگر»; with no later hour that day it says so and keeps the day's list. «Too many open requests» is not treated as a taken hour.
- 2026-10-22: Shorter sign-up (owner approved, item 5; defaults taken): a client's first sign-up screen asks only for the phone number («دریافت کد»); after the texted code come the name and then the password, one field per step («قدم ۲ از ۴»); the terms checkbox is one line under the button «با ادامه دادن، قوانین را می‌پذیرم» (links to قوانین and حریم خصوصی). Consent is implied by continuing; the server has never stored a consent record (the checkbox was only checked in the browser), so nothing recorded changed and a timestamp column is in ROADMAP. Salons and artists keep the one-page form. Without SMS being required the steps are phone, name, password. The sheet on a public salon/artist page uses the same steps.
- 2026-10-22: Code step: «تغییر شماره» is always at the top; a wrong code gives a red field «کد اشتباه است. دوباره بزن» and, from the server's real count (a code dies after 5 wrong tries), «۲ بار دیگر می‌توانی امتحان کنی»; after 2 wrong codes «کد تازه بفرست» appears with a 30-second wait; right code: green tick «شماره تأیید شد», then the next step. Phone and name (never the password or the code) are kept 10 minutes in that tab (sessionStorage) so Back/Forward does not empty the form. Bad number: «شماره درست نیست؛ مثلاً ۰۹۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹». No security rule was weakened (5 tries per code, 12 per phone per 15 minutes, per-phone and per-address send limits and the name-uniqueness check before the SMS are unchanged).
- 2026-10-22: Chosen service survives sign-up (owner approved, item 5b): the services a guest ticks on a public salon page ride along in the `?open=` hand-off (localStorage, up to 6 names); after the code the client is connected, the salon page opens and the booking form opens on those services with the strip «ادامه برای: کوتاهی مو» («ادامه» / «تغییر خدمت»); if a service is gone from the menu no form opens and the strip says «این خدمت دیگر موجود نیست؛ یکی دیگر را انتخاب کن.» with «انتخاب خدمت». Artist pages are not changed (they hand off the artist only).
- 2026-10-22: Back from a salon page (owner approved, item 5c): «پیدا کردن» keeps its search text, results and scroll position for 15 minutes in memory (not across a reload); an empty name search says «نتیجه‌ای نیست؛ اسم دیگری امتحان کن.» (phone and link searches keep their own words).
- 2026-10-22: Speed (owner approved, item 6): the free-times list is kept 5 seconds per salon, day, services and who asks (client or provider) and shared by callers who arrive while it is computed; every booking created, answered, moved or cancelled in this server drops that salon's entries at once, and changes made elsewhere (hours, services, team, another server process) show up within 5 seconds; it only decides what the page shows, a booking is always re-checked under row locks (409 when the hour was just taken). Public salon and artist pages build from one shared answer per 5 seconds (it used to be two full database reads per visit); a salon's edit shows on its public page within 5 seconds.
- 2026-10-22: Slow line (owner approved, item 6c/d): the salon page's bar now says «رزرو» with «قیمت از … تومان»; portfolio pictures are small (360 px) and lazy, grey blocks stand in until they arrive, a picture that fails says «اینترنتت کند است. دوباره تلاش کن» with «دوباره»; the same note appears when the salon's details do not load inside the app. In the booking form, after 3 seconds of waiting for hours it says «ساعت‌ها دیر می‌آیند؛ دارم دوباره امتحان می‌کنم» (and asks once more by itself) with «دوباره», after 10 seconds «الان شلوغ است. چند لحظهٔ دیگر».
- 2026-10-22: The site-wide cap of 200 texted codes per hour is unchanged. When it is reached the code screen says «الان شلوغ است. با رمز وارد شو یا ۱۰ دقیقه بعد کد بگیر.» (with «ورود با رمز»), a per-phone limit keeps its own message, and the admin home lists «کد پیامکی … نزدیک پر شدن است» from 160 codes in the last hour (80%).
- 2026-10-23: UI review answers (owner, from the screenshot review page): the floating round support button is gone; support opens from a small button next to the bell at the top of the home page and from «ارتباط با پشتیبانی» in settings. The client's «منتظر تأیید سالن» strip is a small pill at the top of the home page (it used to sit in the bottom bar and covered the «رزرو» button of a salon's services sheet); on other tabs it sat on page titles, so it shows on home only, and steps aside while a sheet or dialog is open.
- 2026-10-23: Not wanted (owner): turning the salon's automatic request window on app open into a list on the home page («این یه پیامه که زود می‌ره، برپایهٔ اطلاع‌رسانی») and moving the salon's day bookings above the cover picture. Do not propose these again.
- 2026-10-23: Wanted and built (owner): a middle dot next to a number is a comma or words («منوی خدمات (۴ خدمت)», «ارسال درخواست، شنبه ۲ آبان، ساعت ۱۳:۰۰»), because «•» read as a Persian zero; salon notifications show «فردا» instead of the raw date key and «نوبت‌های پیش‌رو» (today and later, no cancelled) instead of «برنامه امروز»; a confirmed booking that has not started reads «تأیید شده», not «در انتظار» («حتما باید درست کار کنه»); a salon's letter avatar is the name's first letter everywhere («سالن رز» is «س»); the break window has a title and says it is one window for every day; a guest who came from a salon's or artist's link sees that place's card above the sign-up form; the booking-history calendar keeps its last line clear of the close button and says «بدون نوبت» instead of «۰ نوبت».
- 2026-10-23: One voice for all copy (owner approved all 7 proposals of the copy page): casual «تو» with «رو», «می‌خوای», «می‌شه», SMS too; one name per thing; errors say what to do; short calm texts on the client's bookings; fewer words on salon screens; light jokes only on empty and success states; wrong texts fixed. Rules and glossary: `docs/COPY.md`. Legal pages and the admin panel keep their own wording.
- 2026-10-25: Hours sheet rebuilt (roadmap 18, owner approved the picture page): the week as seven rows with a switch, a day opens to whole-hour buttons (8 to 23), «همین ساعت برای روزهای دیگه» lets the owner tick the days (open days pre-ticked), and one «ذخیره» saves every changed day in one request (all or none); closing with unsaved changes asks «تغییرها ذخیره نشده. ذخیره کنم؟». Upcoming bookings that would fall outside the new hours are listed before saving and stay as they are («… نوبت سر جاش می‌مونه»). Two working periods in one day (lunch break) is left for later. Vercel previews were failing that day for every branch, so the owner chose: test it several times locally, then send it to the main server.
- 2026-10-24: Where an artist on a salon team receives the clients who book them directly (owner's idea; the owner asked to build it on the test copy and try many cases; defaults below were taken from the recommended answers and are open to change). The ARTIST chooses, in the collab tab under the salon's card («مشتری‌هات کجا نوبت بگیرن؟»): «توی سالن» / «جای خودم» / «هر روز یه جا» (a place per weekday). Until they choose, nothing changes («جای خودم», as before). The salon keeps one switch per member, «نوبت مستقیم توی سالن» (on by default), in the member's manage sheet. At the salon the bookable hours are the hours BOTH are open (and a member on leave or switched off in the team is not there); a booking made there remembers the salon, and the client sees «کجا» with the salon's name and address and the map pin. A day the salon is closed, the artist is away from it, or the salon switched off refuses in plain words (the client keeps the page, can read why and is sent to the salon's own booking when it is switched off). Changing the choice or the switch never moves what is already booked. The salon sees, per member, how many different people booked at the salon this month and how many of them are connected to it, and a list of the upcoming direct visits (when, how long, which artist), never the client's name or number; a client reaches the salon's customers only by connecting. Nobody is connected to the salon automatically. An artist on two teams chooses for one salon (the newest, or the one chosen). When the artist leaves or is removed, or the salon leaves frfro, the direct bookings held at that salon stay and move to the artist's own place, and their clients get a push («جای نوبتت عوض شد»); this is not covered by the 24-hour rule because nothing is cancelled. Migration 047; not built yet and listed in docs/ROADMAP.md: the artist's own owner-side view of where each booking is held, a place note in the SMS texts.
- 2026-10-24 (evening, owner's correction; this REPLACES the three-choice version above, migration 048): the CLIENT never chooses or sees a place: only the service, a day and an hour from the artist's own hours (no tags on days, no refusal for a place). The ARTIST keeps an ordered list of their salons in the collab tab («نوبت مشتری‌هام کجا باشه؟»): add, move up or down, remove; a booking goes to the first salon in the list that can take it. A salon CAN take it when: the member is on duty (not «مرخصی» or «غیرفعال»), the salon's switch «نوبت مستقیم توی سالن» is on, the salon is open that weekday, the whole visit fits inside the salon's hours, and the salon has room that day (its day capacity «ظرفیت روز» from its hours, counting its own live bookings plus the direct ones held there). When none can: the last row of the list, «اگه هیچ‌کدوم جا نداشت», is either «مثل قبل» (default; the booking is held as before the feature) or «بهم خبر بده» (the booking is never auto-confirmed and the artist gets «درخواست تازه، جاش رو بگو», then picks the place). The artist's own place is never named or given an address in the app (owner: it must not be written); a client only reads a place when it is a salon, and only once the booking is confirmed. The artist can move one booking to another of their salons, or back to «مثل قبل», from its sheet («جای نوبت», PUT /api/artist-bookings/:id/place); the client gets a push when a confirmed booking moves. Several salons are supported. A salon that closes direct bookings is skipped (not an error; the artist's list says so). Leaving or removal takes the salon out of the list and holds its bookings «مثل قبل» with the push of before. Version-1 choices ('salon', 'days') were carried over as a one-salon list; 'days' is gone. Capacity is the salon's existing day capacity, no new salon setting.
- 2026-10-24 (location, owner): where the client is told to go is simple: the artist's own pin (settings → جام روی نقشه) normally, and the salon's pin once the booking is confirmed at that salon (`placeLocation` on the client's booking, with the «کجاست؟» map button). - 2026-10-25: Where an artist's own clients go, new model (owner approved all six recommendations on the scenarios page; this REPLACES the 2026-10-24 ordered-salon-list model, which is only on the unmerged test branch): every booking happens either at the artist's own place or at ONE salon, the artist's «محل کار». The salon sees the artist's client bookings as a violet row «مشتری نگار» with time, service and the client's first name; when the artist's clients go to the artist's own place the salon sees only a striped «نگار سرش شلوغه». Bookable hours are only those when both the artist and the workplace salon are open. Leaving a salon works as already built (bookings move to the artist's own place, «جای نوبتت عوض شد»). Not built yet.
