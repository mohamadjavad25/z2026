# frfro: project notes (detail)

How each part of frfro works and why, with the owner's decisions and dates. Moved here from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-30 so it is not read on every session; `CLAUDE.md` keeps one line per area and points here. Read the matching section before touching that area, and update it in the same change when the area changes.

## Areas

- The product name is **frfro**, always lowercase in English. The Persian form is «فرفرو». Never use "Farfaroo", "Frfru", "Zibaban" or «زیبابان».
- Logo files live in `public/brand/`; app icons in `public/icons/`. The app icon (user's design, chosen 2026-10-19) is the mascot peeking up from the bottom: pink curly hair, black face, two white eyes. The source is `public/brand/frfro-app-icon.png` (an AI-made image the user picked, used unchanged), with a traced copy in `frfro-app-icon.svg`; `public/icons/` is resized from that PNG. The Android app (`mobile/android`) uses the same logo since 2026-10-19 (user's request: «only the site logo is right»): launcher icon (adaptive, white background), splash (round logo on white) and the notification icon (`drawable-*/ic_stat_frfro.png`, white silhouette made from the logo), all made from `frfro-app-icon.png`; the iOS shell still shows the old icon. For account pictures shown as a circle (the frfro Gmail, 2026-11-04; also fits Instagram and the like) use `public/brand/frfro-profile.png`: the same image lifted 90 px so both eyes sit inside the circle, the face carried down to the bottom edge (1024×1024).

- Subscriptions (user's decision 2026-10-16, built the same day): clients are free; salons and artists get 7 free days (owner, 2026-11-03; from sign-up, or from the day payment is switched on for older accounts; notices on day five and the last day), then a 1/6/12-month plan (prices in `app/lib/billing/plans.js`). An account is closed (out of the directory and name search, no new online bookings, no new booking entered by hand, no client reminders; it still sees and answers, moves or cancels the bookings it has; nothing deleted; owner 2026-11-03, `app/lib/billing/access.js`) only while billing is on and a gateway is ready. Money rules: amounts come from the plan on the server, "paid" only from the gateway's verify, every write in `app/lib/db/repos/billing.js` under row locks. `/pay` is shown on every screen size (the bank's return page). Operations: `docs/OPERATIONS.md` «اشتراک سالن و آرتیست». Being on a salon's team exempts no artist (user's decision 2026-10-16); a salon may optionally pay a team artist's subscription (`forUserId` at checkout, `payments.payer_user_id`). The refund policy (terms §4) and the 10-year record keeping (privacy §3) are a common, conservative draft the lawyer still has to review; refunds go back through the gateway's panel and are recorded in the admin panel («ثبت بازپرداخت»).

- Money reads in groups of three (owner 2026-11-06: «برای هر سه تا صفر یه خط بکشه»): every amount the app shows or lets someone type has a mark «٬» after each three digits, in Persian digits (`۴۵۰٬۰۰۰`), so the size is clear at a glance and nobody counts zeros. Showing: `formatToman` / `formatTomanNumber` in `app/shared/lib/money.js` (admin: `toman` / `num`; team: `toman` in `teamWeek.js`); never print a raw number as a price. Typing: a money field uses `groupTypedAmount` from the same file in its onChange and `parseTomanAmount` to read it back, never `type="number"` (it cannot show the mark). A service's price is free text (`از ۶۵۰ هزار`): only a plain number is grouped, words are left as typed. Test: `tests/unit/moneyInput.test.js`. Any new money screen or field follows this.
- Service prices and lengths are free text (2026-11-06). People read a price through `servicePriceText` (`ServicePrice` in lists); sums use `plainPriceAmount`, which is 0 for anything without one exact amount, so a visit with «از …» has no total. Length is read by `parseServiceDurationMinutes` (hours, «و نیم», minutes; a range counts as its longer end), the same on the page and on the server. A booking keeps the minutes it was made with; a changed service reaches the next booking within the short caches (15 s directory, 5 s page and free times). `parseTomanAmount` is for number fields only.
- «نمایش قیمت‌ها» (settings → «نمایش برای مشتری‌ها», for artists and salons; owner's decision 2026-11-01): off means nobody but the provider sees a price of theirs. Enforced on the server (`app/lib/db/repos/userSettings.js` `hidesPrices` / `shownServices` / `idsHidingPrices`): for any viewer who is not the provider (owner check by user id on the server), each service comes out with `price: ""` and `priceHidden: true`, in `getPublicArtist`, `getSalon` (also the salon's own `price` tag), `listSalons` (the directory is one answer for everyone, so always hidden there), `listSavedSalonsForUser` and the client's connected cards (`connections.js` `attachServices`); the SSR pages `/artists/[id]` and `/salons/[id]` build from those, and no JSON-LD or metadata carries prices. Whose switch: a salon's own menu (`salon_services`) follows the salon, also when the client picks a team artist on its page; an artist's own menu (`artist_services`), also booked at their workplace salon, follows the artist. The page leaves the price spot empty (no «قیمت توافقی», no total; `ServicesOnPage`, `ServicesDropUp`, `PublicArtistServicesPanel`, `PublicArtistBookingPanel`, `SalonTeam`); the duration still shows. The provider's own workspace (`/api/artist/me`, `/api/salon-services`, the team ledger, their own page while signed in) keeps every price. Bookings never read a price from the client. A change shows within the directory cache (15 s) and the public page cache (5 s). Test: `tests/integration/showPrices.test.js`.

- Feedback and the come-back nudge (owner 2026-11-06, replaces the full-screen welcome survey of 2026-11-05): «نظر و پیشنهاد» is a topic in support (`features/support/FeedbackForm.jsx`, lists in `app/shared/lib/feedback.js`). No voting, no public list, and the text never promises a change. The nudge (`welcomeSurvey*` files keep their names): a salon or artist with 3 posts or 3 services, or a client with a first booking, never in the first 2 hours, who has been away 3 days (`users.last_seen_at`; people the footprint has not seen yet are never pushed) or was already pushed. One push, no name (titles «کار با فرفرو چطور بود؟», salon «ساختن صفحهٔ سالنت چطور بود؟», client «نوبت گرفتن چطور بود؟»), Android app push if they have the app, otherwise browser push (`appFirst` in `sendPushToUser`), otherwise they see it when they open the app (SurveyHost opens the same form). «بعداً» (closing without sending) waits 3 days, the second one ends it; sending ends it (`action: "done"`). No SMS, and no card or push after ordinary actions. The old answers and the admin page «نظرسنجی» stay as history.
