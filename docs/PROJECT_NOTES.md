# frfro: project notes (detail)

How each part of frfro works and why, with the owner's decisions and dates. Moved here from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-30 so it is not read on every session; `CLAUDE.md` keeps one line per area and points here. Read the matching section before touching that area, and update it in the same change when the area changes.

## Areas

- The product name is **frfro**, always lowercase in English. The Persian form is «فرفرو». Never use "Farfaroo", "Frfru", "Zibaban" or «زیبابان».
- Logo files live in `public/brand/`; app icons in `public/icons/`. The app icon (user's design, chosen 2026-10-19) is the mascot peeking up from the bottom: pink curly hair, black face, two white eyes. The source is `public/brand/frfro-app-icon.png` (an AI-made image the user picked, used unchanged), with a traced copy in `frfro-app-icon.svg`; `public/icons/` is resized from that PNG. The Android app (`mobile/android`) uses the same logo since 2026-10-19 (user's request: «only the site logo is right»): launcher icon (adaptive, white background), splash (round logo on white) and the notification icon (`drawable-*/ic_stat_frfro.png`, white silhouette made from the logo), all made from `frfro-app-icon.png`; the iOS shell still shows the old icon.
- Future plans (ideas reviewed but not started) live in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Add new ones there; never start building a plan until the user says so.
- The Android/iOS app shell lives in `mobile/` (Capacitor, opens https://frfro.ir); see `mobile/README.md`. The Android release signing key (`frfro-release.keystore`) is held by the user and is never committed. The APK served by the site is `public/download/frfro.apk`; `/download` is the about/download page, and on tablets and desktops every page except `/admin`, `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/pay` (the bank's return page) shows that page instead (`LargeScreenGate`, user's decision 2026-10-14). On phones the home page `/` also shows that page first, until the visitor taps «ورود به فرفرو» there (remembered in localStorage `frfro-entered`); inside the app and when opened from the home-screen icon `/` opens the app directly. Deep links (salon, artist, `/p/...`) still open directly on phones (user's request 2026-10-14). On tablets and desktops the about page shows a big QR (`ScanToOpen.jsx`) that holds the address of the page the visitor came to, so the phone opens that same page (user's request 2026-10-17). **Downloads closed (user's decision 2026-10-21, while the app waits for Cafe Bazaar's review; the APK on the site is not right):** `APP_DOWNLOADS_OPEN = false` in `app/features/landing/constants.js`. While off: no download link anywhere; the sign-up and login screens say «به‌زودی: اپلیکیشن و وبلاگ فرفرو»; phones open the app at `/` (no about page first) and `/download` on a phone opens the app; the about page shows on tablets and desktops only, with «اپلیکیشن فرفرو به‌زودی» in place of the download buttons and "add to home screen" steps in its install section. Turn it back on (one line) only when the user says the app is published.
- The public website (the about/download page in `app/features/landing/`, styles in `app/styles/features/landing.css`) is Claude's to own and design end to end (user's decision 2026-10-14); the user works on the app itself. Its look is "ink & light" (user's request 2026-10-15: less pink, closer to the app): white and ink (`#0c0d12`) sections that alternate, with the mascot's pink (`#ff86b8`) and a violet (`#8b6cff`) blended together (`--l-blend`) for emphasis words, glows and cards, never one colour for a whole section. Type is Vazirmatn only, and the mascot images are in `public/brand/mascot/`. Keep the copy in plain Persian, and keep the download link and «ورود به فرفرو» working. Its «آموزش» section (user's request 2026-10-15) is built but hidden since the same day (user's decision: visitors got confused when the real app looked different); it is not mounted in `AppLanding`. In its place the hero shows one silent vertical (9:16) film (`HeroFilm.jsx`, `public/video/`, how it is made: `docs/hero-film/README.md`) with no app screens and no mascot (user's decision 2026-10-15); its photos are AI-made and live in `public/brand/photos/`. The hidden section holds short silent tutorials for clients, salons and artists: not video files but scripts (`app/features/landing/tutorials.js`) played on a mock phone with a tapping finger (`LearnSection.jsx`, `TutorialScreen.jsx`, styles in `app/styles/features/landingLearn.css`). The mock screens use the app's real button labels, so when a flow or label in the app changes, update its tutorial too.
- Colour system (user's decision 2026-10-15, Claude designed it end to end): four brand colours (ink `#0c0d12`, pink `#ff86b8`, violet `#8b6cff`, white), a neutral ramp that flips at night, day/night/auto theme (`data-theme`), and a role accent (`data-role`: pink for clients, violet for salons and artists). The full rules are in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` («سیستم رنگ»); tokens live in `app/styles/tokens.css`, dark islands in `app/styles/islands.css`. Do not add hard-coded colours to app CSS. No black buttons anywhere in the app (user's decision 2026-10-27): violet for what you do for your own business or account, pink (with ink text) for anything done with a client or a booking, and what is new or live is pink for every role (`app/styles/features/brandLive.css`). In the native shell (`mobile/`) the status bar is still `#1c202c`; the Android icon and splash follow the logo (see project notes).
- Service icons were redrawn from scratch on 2026-10-15 on the brand palette (`app/shared/constants/beautyArt/inkLight.js`). The rules for new or changed icons are in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` («آیکن خدمات»). Icon ids are stored in the database: never rename or remove one.
- Subscriptions (user's decision 2026-10-16, built the same day): clients are free; salons and artists get 30 free days, then a 1/6/12-month plan (prices in `app/lib/billing/plans.js`). Billing is switched on in the admin panel («پرداخت‌ها»); the gateway is `FRFRO_PAYMENT_GATEWAY` (`sandbox` for trying, `zarinpal` ready, others are one file in `app/lib/payments`). An account is closed (out of the directory and name search, no new online bookings, nothing deleted) only while billing is on and a gateway is ready. Money rules: amounts come from the plan on the server, "paid" only from the gateway's verify, every write in `app/lib/db/repos/billing.js` under row locks. `/pay` is shown on every screen size (the bank's return page). Operations: `docs/OPERATIONS.md` «اشتراک سالن و آرتیست». Being on a salon's team exempts no artist (user's decision 2026-10-16); a salon may optionally pay a team artist's subscription (`forUserId` at checkout, `payments.payer_user_id`). The refund policy (terms §4) and the 10-year record keeping (privacy §3) are a common, conservative draft the lawyer still has to review; refunds go back through the gateway's panel and are recorded in the admin panel («ثبت بازپرداخت»).
- Connecting (built 2026-10-18 on the user's request to make salon/artist/client connections effortless): the one word in the UI is «وصل شدن» («وصل شو» / «وصل هستی»), never «دنبال کردن», though the data is still the `follows` table. A public salon/artist page's «رزرو» opens the app on that same place (`/?open=salon-12`, `app/shared/lib/appHandoff.js`): a guest goes straight to the client sign-up form, then is connected and lands on its services; `?open=` and `?join=` skip the about page. Once connected, the button says «وصل هستی» for two seconds, then turns into a linked look whose menu holds «قطع اتصال» and «گزارش به پشتیبانی» (opens a support complaint titled with that page's name; `features/connect/ConnectToggle.jsx`, user's request 2026-10-21). Salons and artists bring their customers through «دعوت مشتری» on the customers page (`InviteClientsSheet.jsx`: counter QR, ready message, scan a client's code).
- Customers list and names (2026-10-31, owner's design after seeing one phone booked under two names): the customers page keeps ONE card per phone (last ten digits, `app/features/salons/customers.js`) and remembers every name on it: `names` (each with its own counts), the main name first, `otherNames` shown as pink chips (two, then «+۳»); the sheet lists each name with its own count and puts the name in front of each booking. Main name: the account's own name if the number has a frfro account, else the name used most, newest on a tie. Names are the same when they match by `nameKey` (spaces, case and Arabic letter forms ignored); «مریم» and «مریم جون» are two. Search finds the card by any of its names or the number (`customerMatchesQuery`; the booking form's suggestions list every name too). The bookings lists stay as they were, one card per booking, and a booking whose number is booked under another name gets the tag «هم‌شماره با …» (`buildSharedPhoneLookup`, `ScheduleRow` `sharedWith`). When a salon or artist saves a booking with a name that is new for a known number, `NameConflictSheet` asks first (`findNameConflict`, only once everything else is filled in; not on editing a booking): «هر دو باشن» (main button: saved under the new name), «همون اسم قبلی بمونه», or «اسم رو عوض کن» (asks again, then `POST /api/customers/rename` renames every one of the owner's bookings for that number, also the copies its team artists see, never one tied to a frfro account; no database change). A number with a frfro account takes the account's name and the owner is only told. The tile «مشتری» counts numbers. Not unified yet: the artist-side visit dots (`buildClientHistoryLookup`) still join rows by number OR name OR account (HEALTH debt).
- «کجاست؟» (user's request 2026-10-18): on a salon's or artist's public page a square pin button at the start of the buttons row, beside «وصل شو» (`components/LocationButton.jsx` `variant="action"`, moved from next to the name 2026-10-27, styles `app/styles/features/location.css`) opens a sheet with only the map and the pinned spot (user's decision 2026-10-21: no address card, no copy, no Balad, routing or taxi buttons; the Tapsi work is parked on branch `claude/tapsi-ride`, not merged). Without a pin there is no button. In the client's booking details «کجا» is a card (`LocationCard`, same file) with a small still map, the place's name and area and «روی نقشه»; tapping it opens the same sheet, which is the map filling the sheet with the name floating on top, the name on the pin, and «برگرد سر پین» and zoom buttons (redesigned 2026-10-26 on the user's request «خیلی بهتر»). Salon bookings carry the salon's pin too (`placeLocation`). The pin and plate/floor come from تنظیمات → ثبت لوکیشن (`users.geo_lat/geo_lng/address_detail`, migration `036`).
- Hosting is moving from Vercel + Supabase to the user's own server (user's decision 2026-10-18): how to deploy there is in `docs/DEPLOY_SERVER.md` (files in `deploy/`, scripts in `scripts/`), what is still open before launch in `docs/LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`. The Vercel project stays as a test copy; only the domain and the data move (user's decision 2026-10-19). Every later move between servers goes through `docs/SERVER_MOVE.md` (`scripts/move-export.sh` / `move-import.sh`, row counts checked, short maintenance window, old server forwards to the new one), so moving never loses data (user's request 2026-10-19). The app keeps nothing on disk besides the database, the env file and the certificates: keep it that way, or add the new thing to the move bundle.
- Migrations (`migrations/NNN_name.sql`): every Vercel deploy, previews of unmerged branches included, runs `npm run migrate` against the one shared database, so the database can already hold another branch's migration. `scripts/migrate.mjs` therefore runs "up" without node-pg-migrate's order check (it compared run and local migrations position by position and failed every other branch's build, 2026-10-18). Still: before adding a migration, `git fetch origin` and take a number above every migration on main **and** on other open branches, and keep each migration independent of other branches' (only its own columns/tables, `IF NOT EXISTS`).
- Push in the Android app (built 2026-10-19 on the user's request to save SMS): `@capacitor/push-notifications` + Firebase (`app/lib/fcm.js`, tokens in `native_push_tokens`, site side `app/shared/lib/nativePush.js`). Rule (user's decision): someone with the Android app and notifications on gets booking/subscription news as a push only, unless that push failed to reach Firebase (then the SMS goes too, `pushReachedApp` in `app/lib/push.js`, 2026-10-22); everyone else, iPhone and browser users included, also gets the SMS. It switches on only once the user makes the Firebase project (`docs/OPERATIONS.md` «اعلان اپ اندروید»). The app version stays 1.0.0 (user's request 2026-10-19): do not raise `versionName`. A second, private app «فرفرو مدیر» (`ir.frfro.admin`, Gradle flavor `admin`, opens `/admin`, never published in a store; user's choice 2026-10-19) gets the operator's news as a push instead of the admin SMS (`app/lib/adminPush.js`, tokens in `admin_push_tokens`); its icon is the logo on red with red eyes (`public/brand/frfro-admin-icon.png`). See `mobile/README.md` «اپ مدیر».
- Names (user's decision 2026-10-20): a salon or artist name is one per account across salons and artists; clients are real people and may share a name with anyone. "Same name" ignores spaces, emoji, punctuation, case and Arabic/Persian letter forms (`app/lib/businessName.js`). It is checked at sign-up (also before the SMS code is sent), on profile renames and on the admin's name fix; duplicates from before the rule are left as they are and can still save their profile.
- App release: the hold of 2026-10-14 was lifted by the user on 2026-10-19 to publish on Cafe Bazaar and Myket (Google Play later). The signing key of 2026-10-14 was lost, so a new one was made that day (fingerprint in `mobile/README.md` and `public/.well-known/assetlinks.json`); the user holds it, it is never committed. The version stays 1.0.0 (see above).

## Vercel env rename (2026-10-10, done)

- The `ZIBABAN_*` → `FRFRO_*` environment variable rename in Vercel (project `z2026`) is done and deployed on 2026-10-10. The secrets (`FRFRO_ADMIN_SECRET`, `FRFRO_ADMIN_SETUP_KEY`, `FRFRO_ADMIN_TOKEN`, `FRFRO_VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`) were regenerated, not copied, because Vercel does not expose sensitive values.
- Because `FRFRO_ADMIN_SECRET` changed, each admin had to re-enroll their authenticator once via "راه‌اندازی اولیه" on `/admin` (supported since PR #109). The user confirmed on 2026-10-10 that they re-enrolled and can log in to the admin panel. Push subscriptions must be re-enabled by users; push was not verified. Do not nag the user about this at session start.
- The old `ZIBABAN_*` variables still exist in Vercel and are harmless; the user may delete them by hand.


- Story maker and the calm story card (2026-10-31, owner's request, built for testing on Vercel first): `InstagramStorySheet` has a «شخصی‌سازی» button (the sliders icon beside the main button) opening one panel with three tabs: «ساعت‌ها» (any free time can be switched on or off, at most 3 per day), «متن» (four ready headlines or a typed one, up to 28 characters, split into two balanced lines of one size) and «پس‌زمینه» (شب، صورتی، بنفش، نمونه‌کار). The last text and ground are remembered per phone (`frfro-story-opts`). `StoryNudgeCard` is ONE quiet row on an owner's home, shown only when the profile is complete (the completeness card has the slot until then) and today or tomorrow has free times a client could still request: «امروز ۳ وقت خالی داری»; when a cancelled booking's time is free again it says «نوبت ساعت ۱۸:۰۰ لغو شد و هنوز خالیه» (salons only for now: an artist's cancelled bookings are not in the list the home holds). «×» hides it until tomorrow (`frfro-story-nudge-day`). It never sends a push, never posts by itself and adds nothing to the bell's count: calm on purpose.
