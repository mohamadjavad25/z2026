# frfro: project notes (detail)

How each part of frfro works and why, with the owner's decisions and dates. Moved here from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-30 so it is not read on every session; `CLAUDE.md` keeps one line per area and points here. Read the matching section before touching that area, and update it in the same change when the area changes.

## Areas

- The product name is **frfro**, always lowercase in English. The Persian form is «فرفرو». Never use "Farfaroo", "Frfru", "Zibaban" or «زیبابان».
- Logo files live in `public/brand/`; app icons in `public/icons/`. The app icon (user's design, chosen 2026-10-19) is the mascot peeking up from the bottom: pink curly hair, black face, two white eyes. The source is `public/brand/frfro-app-icon.png` (an AI-made image the user picked, used unchanged), with a traced copy in `frfro-app-icon.svg`; `public/icons/` is resized from that PNG. The Android app (`mobile/android`) uses the same logo since 2026-10-19 (user's request: «only the site logo is right»): launcher icon (adaptive, white background), splash (round logo on white) and the notification icon (`drawable-*/ic_stat_frfro.png`, white silhouette made from the logo), all made from `frfro-app-icon.png`; the iOS shell still shows the old icon. For account pictures shown as a circle (the frfro Gmail, 2026-11-04; also fits Instagram and the like) use `public/brand/frfro-profile.png`: the same image lifted 90 px so both eyes sit inside the circle, the face carried down to the bottom edge (1024×1024).
- Future plans (ideas reviewed but not started) live in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Add new ones there; never start building a plan until the user says so.
- The Android/iOS app shell lives in `mobile/` (Capacitor, opens https://frfro.ir); see `mobile/README.md`. The Android release signing key (`frfro-release.keystore`) is held by the user and is never committed. The APK served by the site is `public/download/frfro.apk`; `/download` is the about/download page, and on tablets and desktops every page except `/admin`, `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/pay` (the bank's return page) shows that page instead (`LargeScreenGate`, user's decision 2026-10-14). On phones the home page `/` also shows that page first, until the visitor taps «ورود به فرفرو» there (remembered in localStorage `frfro-entered`); inside the app and when opened from the home-screen icon `/` opens the app directly. Deep links (salon, artist, `/p/...`) still open directly on phones (user's request 2026-10-14). On tablets and desktops the about page shows a big QR (`ScanToOpen.jsx`) that holds the address of the page the visitor came to, so the phone opens that same page (user's request 2026-10-17). **Downloads closed (user's decision 2026-10-21, while the app waits for Cafe Bazaar's review; the APK on the site is not right):** `APP_DOWNLOADS_OPEN = false` in `app/features/landing/constants.js`. While off: no download link anywhere; the sign-up and login screens say «به‌زودی: اپلیکیشن و وبلاگ فرفرو»; phones open the app at `/` (no about page first) and `/download` on a phone opens the app; the about page shows on tablets and desktops only, with «اپلیکیشن فرفرو به‌زودی» in place of the download buttons and "add to home screen" steps in its install section. Turn it back on (one line) only when the user says the app is published.

- Subscriptions (user's decision 2026-10-16, built the same day): clients are free; salons and artists get 7 free days (owner, 2026-11-03; from sign-up, or from the day payment is switched on for older accounts; notices on day five and the last day), then a 1/6/12-month plan (prices in `app/lib/billing/plans.js`). Billing is switched on in the admin panel («پرداخت‌ها»); the gateway is `FRFRO_PAYMENT_GATEWAY` (`sandbox` for trying, `zarinpal` ready, others are one file in `app/lib/payments`). An account is closed (out of the directory and name search, no new online bookings, no new booking entered by hand, no client reminders; it still sees and answers, moves or cancels the bookings it has; nothing deleted; owner 2026-11-03, `app/lib/billing/access.js`) only while billing is on and a gateway is ready. Money rules: amounts come from the plan on the server, "paid" only from the gateway's verify, every write in `app/lib/db/repos/billing.js` under row locks. `/pay` is shown on every screen size (the bank's return page). Operations: `docs/OPERATIONS.md` «اشتراک سالن و آرتیست». Being on a salon's team exempts no artist (user's decision 2026-10-16); a salon may optionally pay a team artist's subscription (`forUserId` at checkout, `payments.payer_user_id`). The refund policy (terms §4) and the 10-year record keeping (privacy §3) are a common, conservative draft the lawyer still has to review; refunds go back through the gateway's panel and are recorded in the admin panel («ثبت بازپرداخت»).

- Admin support inbox (owner 2026-11-05): every row says in words whose turn it is, from `turnOf` in `app/admin/format.js`: «منتظر پاسخ تو» (amber), «جواب دادی» (green; «پیام دادی» when the admin started the chat) or «بسته». - Footprint «رد پا» (owner 2026-11-05): who came, when, what they opened and did, when they left, for the admin panel only. Tables `activity_visits` and `activity_events` (migration 069). The phone sends small batches to `/api/activity` (`app/shared/lib/activity.js`, mounted by `app/components/ActivityTracker.jsx`): at load, every 30 s if something happened, a ping each minute while the screen is on, and on leaving. A new visit starts after 30 minutes away or on sign-out. Only fixed names go out: nothing typed, no photo, no place, no phone. A visitor is a random id on the phone; when that phone signs in, its earlier visits join the account. The account always comes from the session. Main actions are noted where they happen: path list `ACTIONS` in `app/shared/api/client.js`, artist services in `app/shared/api/artists.js`, «رزرو» taps on the two public pages, link sharing, tutorial end or skip. A new action needs its label in `app/admin/activityLabels.js`. Admin: user page › «رد پا» (`UserFootprint.jsx`), reports › «کجا می‌مونن» (`ActivityReport.jsx`). The owner's test numbers are in `FRFRO_TEST_PHONES` (server env only; the repo is public) and are left out of the report. Detail is kept for good while the account lives («حکم طلا رو دارن»); `serverWatch` pushes «رد پای کاربرها جای زیادی گرفته» at 2 GB, once in 30 days; nothing deletes by itself. A deleted account keeps only numbers with no person: `user_id` goes and a trigger sets `visitor_id` to `gone`. This batch also writes `users.last_seen_at`, which nothing wrote before, so the admin's last seen, online and daily numbers work from the deploy on. Footprint starts on deploy day; nothing earlier can be recovered. The admin panel itself is not tracked. A phone visitor still on the about page shows as «صفحهٔ خانه», because the app behind it is already mounted. Limits on `/api/activity`: 16 KB a batch, 300 rows a minute per address, 2000 rows a visit; when the database has a queue, batches are dropped. Every limit on `/api/activity` is in memory, and a failed batch is dropped: it must never cost a booking.
