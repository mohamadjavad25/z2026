## Project notes

- The product name is **frfro**, always lowercase in English. The Persian form is «فرفرو». Never use "Farfaroo", "Frfru", "Zibaban" or «زیبابان».
- Logo files live in `public/brand/`; app icons in `public/icons/`.
- Future plans (ideas reviewed but not started) live in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Add new ones there; never start building a plan until the user says so.
- The Android/iOS app shell lives in `mobile/` (Capacitor, opens https://frfro.ir); see `mobile/README.md`. The Android release signing key (`frfro-release.keystore`) is held by the user and is never committed. The APK served by the site is `public/download/frfro.apk`; `/download` is the about/download page, and on tablets and desktops every page except `/admin`, `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/pay` (the bank's return page) shows that page instead (`LargeScreenGate`, user's decision 2026-10-14). On phones the home page `/` also shows that page first, until the visitor taps «ورود به فرفرو» there (remembered in localStorage `frfro-entered`); inside the app and when opened from the home-screen icon `/` opens the app directly. Deep links (salon, artist, `/p/...`) still open directly on phones (user's request 2026-10-14). On tablets and desktops the about page shows a big QR (`ScanToOpen.jsx`) that holds the address of the page the visitor came to, so the phone opens that same page (user's request 2026-10-17).
- The public website (the about/download page in `app/features/landing/`, styles in `app/styles/features/landing.css`) is Claude's to own and design end to end (user's decision 2026-10-14); the user works on the app itself. Its look is "ink & light" (user's request 2026-10-15: less pink, closer to the app): white and ink (`#0c0d12`) sections that alternate, with the mascot's pink (`#ff86b8`) and a violet (`#8b6cff`) blended together (`--l-blend`) for emphasis words, glows and cards, never one colour for a whole section. Type is Vazirmatn only, and the mascot images are in `public/brand/mascot/`. Keep the copy in plain Persian, and keep the download link and «ورود به فرفرو» working. Its «آموزش» section (user's request 2026-10-15) is built but hidden since the same day (user's decision: visitors got confused when the real app looked different); it is not mounted in `AppLanding`. In its place the hero shows one silent vertical (9:16) film (`HeroFilm.jsx`, `public/video/`, how it is made: `docs/hero-film/README.md`) with no app screens and no mascot (user's decision 2026-10-15); its photos are AI-made and live in `public/brand/photos/`. The hidden section holds short silent tutorials for clients, salons and artists: not video files but scripts (`app/features/landing/tutorials.js`) played on a mock phone with a tapping finger (`LearnSection.jsx`, `TutorialScreen.jsx`, styles in `app/styles/features/landingLearn.css`). The mock screens use the app's real button labels, so when a flow or label in the app changes, update its tutorial too.
- Colour system (user's decision 2026-10-15, Claude designed it end to end): four brand colours (ink `#0c0d12`, pink `#ff86b8`, violet `#8b6cff`, white), a neutral ramp that flips at night, day/night/auto theme (`data-theme`), and a role accent (`data-role`: pink for clients, violet for salons and artists). The full rules are in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` («سیستم رنگ»); tokens live in `app/styles/tokens.css`, dark islands in `app/styles/islands.css`. Do not add hard-coded colours to app CSS. The native shell (`mobile/`: status bar, splash and icon colours still `#1c202c`) is deliberately untouched until release resumes.
- Service icons were redrawn from scratch on 2026-10-15 on the brand palette (`app/shared/constants/beautyArt/inkLight.js`). The rules for new or changed icons are in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` («آیکن خدمات»). Icon ids are stored in the database: never rename or remove one.
- Subscriptions (user's decision 2026-10-16, built the same day): clients are free; salons and artists get 30 free days, then a 1/6/12-month plan (prices in `app/lib/billing/plans.js`). Billing is switched on in the admin panel («پرداخت‌ها»); the gateway is `FRFRO_PAYMENT_GATEWAY` (`sandbox` for trying, `zarinpal` ready, others are one file in `app/lib/payments`). An account is closed (out of the directory and name search, no new online bookings, nothing deleted) only while billing is on and a gateway is ready. Money rules: amounts come from the plan on the server, "paid" only from the gateway's verify, every write in `app/lib/db/repos/billing.js` under row locks. `/pay` is shown on every screen size (the bank's return page). Operations: `docs/OPERATIONS.md` «اشتراک سالن و آرتیست». Being on a salon's team exempts no artist (user's decision 2026-10-16); a salon may optionally pay a team artist's subscription (`forUserId` at checkout, `payments.payer_user_id`). The refund policy (terms §4) and the 10-year record keeping (privacy §3) are a common, conservative draft the lawyer still has to review; refunds go back through the gateway's panel and are recorded in the admin panel («ثبت بازپرداخت»).
- «کجاست؟» (user's request 2026-10-18): on a salon's or artist's public page a pin button next to the name (`components/LocationButton.jsx`, styles `app/styles/features/location.css`) opens the exact address on a map with «درخواست اسنپ» and Google Maps / Balad / Neshan (links in `app/shared/lib/mapLinks.js`). The pin and plate/floor come from تنظیمات → ثبت لوکیشن (`users.geo_lat/geo_lng/address_detail`, migration `036`). Snapp has no documented link that fills the destination, so its button copies the address and opens Snapp; if Snapp ever documents one, change `SNAPP_URL` there.
- App release is on hold (user's decision 2026-10-14): until the user says the product is complete and tested, do not build signed releases, replace `public/download/frfro.apk`, change `ANDROID_APP_VERSION`, bump the app version, or work on store publishing (Cafe Bazaar, Myket, Google Play). The version in `mobile/android/app/build.gradle` reads 1.1.0 but nothing past 1.0.0 was ever released.
